# Jet Island Archipelago

## What does randomization do to this game?
In Jet Island, the modifiers, abilities, and stats are randomized

## What is the goal of Jet Island when randomized?
The goal of Jet Island is to unlock all abilities and defeat the worm

## Which items can be in another player's world?
All modifiers, abilities, and stats can be found in another player's world.

## What does another world's item look like in Jet Island?
All items appearance remain unchanged, but have been added to the minimap for ease of use

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
if you receive a modifier it will be applied automatically, otherwise the ability or stat is added automatically.
