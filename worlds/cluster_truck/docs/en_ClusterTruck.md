# ClusterTruck Archipelago

## What does randomization do to this game?

In ClusterTruck, the abilities and levels are randomized

## What is the goal of ClusterTruck when randomized?
The goal of randomized ClusterTruck is to complete a set amount of levels and beat the goal level you select

## Which items can be in another player's world?
All levels and abilities can be found in another player's world.

## What does another world's item look like in ClusterTruck?
Items in the abilities shop will show up as the name of the item from the other world

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
If you receive an ability it appears in the ability menu, if you receive a level it will show up in the level select menu
